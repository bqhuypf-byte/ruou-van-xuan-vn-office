Small rounded pill for status, category, and stock labels on cards, tables, and product detail.

```jsx
<Badge variant="success">Đang Bán</Badge>
```

Variants map to real usage: `primary` (category tag on product cards), `success` (in-stock / active), `danger` (out-of-stock / discount %/ inactive), `warning`, `info`, `default` (color/size chips on cart line items). Always pilled (`border-radius: full`), never square.
