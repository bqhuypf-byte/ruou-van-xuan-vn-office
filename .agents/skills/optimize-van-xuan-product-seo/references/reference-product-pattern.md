# Reference product pattern

This reference records the user-supplied screenshot of **Rượu Nếp Vạn Xuân 1
Lít**. Use it as a page/content pattern only. Re-read live data before treating
any observed value as current or factual.

## Observed presentation

- Breadcrumb: category and volume path leading to the product.
- Clear product name followed by current price and crossed-out comparison price.
- Alcohol-level choices displayed as separate selectable variants: 30°, 40°,
  and 50°.
- Availability and SKU shown near the selected variant.
- Primary purchase action, add-to-cart action, shipping, returns, and wholesale
  contact blocks.
- Main product image plus a gallery.
- Description and review tabs.
- Description begins with a product-positioning section and then supporting
  paragraphs/bullets about process and product commitments.

## Reusable content blueprint

1. Product identity: `[type] + Vạn Xuân + [volume/meaningful variant]`.
2. Short summary: one natural sentence stating only verified product type,
   material/process, sensory character, and intended context.
3. Long description:
   - concise opening that answers what the product is;
   - verified highlights specific to this product;
   - ingredients/material and production method when documented;
   - aroma/taste/serving notes when documented;
   - volume and alcohol variants sourced from SKU data;
   - storage/use guidance when documented;
   - responsible-use wording appropriate for alcohol.
4. Commercial and operational details remain in variant/site-setting fields,
   not repeated as fragile prose when they can change frequently.

## Defects not to reproduce

The screenshot contains editor/AI-instruction text beginning with wording similar
to “Chào bạn, dựa trên những thông tin chi tiết...” and a parenthetical editing
instruction. Remove such meta-commentary from the public description. Also
verify the apparent mismatch between the main artwork mentioning “40 độ” and
the selected 30° variant before reusing that image/variant relationship.

Do not assume the displayed price, stock count, SKU, hotline, delivery promise,
return period, composition claims, or “không pha cồn/không hóa chất” claims are
valid for another product. Read their Admin-managed sources first.
